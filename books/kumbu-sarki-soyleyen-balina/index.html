Kumbu: Şarkı Söyleyen Balina

Gülçağ Konçe

Deep beneath the sea, marine creatures encounter Kumbu, a mysterious sperm whale who continues to swim to his own rhythm despite the waste and murky waters around him. Drawn to his serenity and enchanting song, they follow him and unwittingly set a remarkable transformation in motion. Written and illustrated by Gülçağ Konçe, Kumbu: The Singing Whale is a hopeful picture book about hearing our inner voice amid the noise of the outside world and discovering how personal change can transform our surroundings. Through a playful exploration of pollution and consumption, it shows that even discarded objects can be given new life.
