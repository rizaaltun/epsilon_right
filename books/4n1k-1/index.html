4N1K 1

Büşra Yılmaz

Series synopsis: 4N1K is the witty and heartwarming story of five inseparable friends; four boys and one girl. Yaprak grew up as an ordinary kid, but when she’s with her four wild and loyal friends, she’s anything but ordinary. Her world revolves around her dimpled hero Ali; Oğuz, the lunatic whose only obsession is photo booths and flirting; and Sinan, a quirky sock-fetishist whose grades never quite add up to 100. For twelve years, these boys have filled her life with laughter, loyalty, and color. With them by her side, Yaprak neither believes in nor needs love. But everything changes on her birthday, when a mysterious package arrives and turns her world upside down.
