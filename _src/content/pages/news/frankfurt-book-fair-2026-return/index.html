Frankfurt, Here We Come: Epsilon Publishing Group Returns for FBM26

Editorial desk·8 September 2026

Two years have flown by since our very first Frankfurt stand in 2024. Last year we added a little extra sparkle with a networking cocktail — and this year, in our third year, we are returning stronger, more excited and more ready than ever to meet you all again.

Find us at Hall 5.1, Stand C20 from 7–11 October 2026. Pelin Yılmaz and Merve Sarıhan will be on site all week with the full autumn list — 135 titles across 88 territories — for anyone who wants to talk rights.

Come for the books, stay for the conversation.
