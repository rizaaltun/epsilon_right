Publishers Weekly Reports on Sharjah’s Global Bookselling Gathering

28 September 2026 · Press roundup

The fifth Sharjah Booksellers Conference welcomed 912 participants representing 99 countries. Publishers Weekly reports on the international gathering and its focus on the future of bookselling.

The event took place at Expo Centre Sharjah on 19–20 September 2026. Its programme explored how bookshops can reach new readers and strengthen their businesses.
