Epsilon Publishing Group

Turkish stories, travelling the world

Founded in 1993, Epsilon Publishing Group is one of Türkiye's leading publishing groups. Since 2019 we have been representing our authors and home-grown titles in international markets — and today the rights to more than 100 of our titles have been sold in almost 70 languages across 180 territories.

Since 2024 we have welcomed the international publishing community at our own stands at the Bologna Children's Book Fair, Frankfurt Book Fair, and Shanghai International Children's Book Fair.
